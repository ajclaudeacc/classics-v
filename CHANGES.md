# What changed from classicsfive.com

Compared against the live site as of November 13, 2026. "New wording" marks text written for the redesign that does not
appear on the current site; everything else is the original content, sometimes shortened or lightly edited.

## Site-wide

### Platform and look
- Rebuilt from Squarespace to a custom Next.js site, keeping every page at its current address (`/weddings`,
  `/banquetfacilities`, `/meetings`, `/funeral-gatherings`, `/drop-off`, `/about`, `/classicsfivephotos`, `/contact`).
- Colors taken from the Classics V logo (steel navy and deep ink on white), replacing the mix of script fonts, black-and-white
  banner photos and decorative swirl dividers.
- New fonts: Marcellus (headings) and Nunito Sans (text), replacing the script and serif fonts.
- Removed: the five script-text banner images (Funeral Gatherings, Banquets & Showers, Seminar & Meetings, Wedding
  Receptions, Catering Menu), the swirl divider graphics, the WeddingWire logo image and "Powered by Squarespace".
- Large color photo headers on every page instead of the black-and-white building photo.
- New browser-tab icon.

### Navigation and header
- Original menu: Home, Banquets & Showers, Weddings, Meetings, Funeral Gatherings, Contact, About, Photos (all capitals).
- New menu: Weddings, Banquets & Showers, Meetings, Funeral Gatherings, Catering, Our Rooms, Photos.
  - Weddings moved first.
  - **Catering added to the menu.** The page existed (`/drop-off`) but was only reachable from a homepage banner.
  - "About" renamed **"Our Rooms"**, since the page is mostly room details.
  - "Home" is now the logo; "Contact" is now the **"Book a tour"** button, plus the mobile menu and footer.
- Phone number and a "Book a tour" button added to the header on every page.
- New mobile menu with a full-width "Call (716) 691-6000" button.
- Phone number written as (716) 691-6000 instead of (716)691-6000.

### Footer
- Original: "Classics Five Banquet and Conference Center, 2425 Niagara Falls Blvd, Amherst, NY 14228, Contact us at
  (716)691-6000" plus social icons.
- New: the name as "Classics V Banquet & Conference Center", the tagline "Personal Service with a Classical Touch",
  "Family owned and operated since 1985", the address linked to Google Maps, phone, email, "Evenings and weekends by
  appointment only", links to every page, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and "Reviews on WeddingWire", the service-area line
  and a copyright line.

### Repeated text removed
- The city lists ("Buffalo, NY | Amherst, NY | Cheektowaga, NY…" and "We proudly serve Amherst, NY; North Tonawanda, NY…")
  that appeared several times per page now appear once: a small line in each page header and one line in the footer.

### Preview-only additions (remove at launch)
- A notice at the top: "Design preview for Classics V. The official website is classicsfive.com. To book, call
  (716) 691-6000."
- Hidden from search engines while in preview.
- Forms validate but do not send anything until switched on, and show a message pointing to the phone and email instead.

### Accessibility and usability
- Descriptive alt text on every photo (the original used file names like "IMG_3074.jpg" or none).
- Visible keyboard focus, a "Skip to main content" link, one main heading per page, labeled form fields, errors shown next
  to each field, and tap targets of at least 44 pixels.
- Videos show a thumbnail and load only when played, instead of loading YouTube on page load.
- Menu downloads are clear cards that say "PDF".
- Page titles and search descriptions rewritten for each page.
